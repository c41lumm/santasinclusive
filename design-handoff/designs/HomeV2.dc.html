Our story

It began with our son, Hudson.

In 2022 we opened the first grotto so that children with disabilities could meet Santa without crowds, noise or a ticking clock.

It has grown into one of the largest inclusive Christmas experiences in the world. It is still funded by our family every year, we buy every gift ourselves, and a team who care deeply about getting it right helps us make each visit special.
