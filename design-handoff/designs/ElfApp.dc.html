Elf web app, phone

Everything the team needs, one thumb away

Each volunteer logs in on their own phone. What they see depends on their role, so try switching below.

  • Next up: the visitor about to arrive, how they like to meet Santa, what they love and what to avoid. Mark them arrived, with Santa, then done.
  • Today: every visit for the day with its status, so the whole team knows where things stand.
  • Gifts: tap "Given" as each gift is handed over, and check in deliveries, counting what really arrived. Both update the admin stock straight away.
  • Rota: each person's own shifts and who they are working with, plus a button to say they can't make one.
  • Santa's view: just the essentials in large type: name, how to say hello, what they love, what to avoid, who is with them and the gift. One big button moves to the next visitor.
  • Photos: pick who is in the photo first. Only families who agreed to sharing can go to Instagram.
