About your company

How would you like to pay?

Santa's Inclusive Grotto is not a registered charity, so sponsorship is not a charitable donation and Gift Aid does not apply. You will receive a receipt. Please ask your accountant how sponsorship is treated for your company. Sponsorship is received by Liquid New Media Limited, which runs the grotto.

Where your sponsorship goes

100% of sponsorship is spent on toys for the children.

If any funds are left over after Christmas, they will be donated to Menphys, a children's charity.
